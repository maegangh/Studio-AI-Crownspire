## 📅 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & LIVEOPS PHILOSOPHY
This document details the **Year 1 Live Operations Plan** for **Crownspire**. Our LiveOps strategy is designed to balance continuous player engagement with structured, high-value cosmetic and tactical progression milestones. 

### Core KPIs & Engagement Goals
1.  **D30 Retention Booster:** Utilizing alternating 3-day short-cycle skirmishes and 7-day server-wide world events to keep queue times short and active alliances constant.
2.  **Monetization Vectors:** Introducing premium cosmetic **Castle Skins**, exclusive **Companion Eggs**, and seasonal **Sovereign Commanders** tied directly to seasonal Battle Passes ("The Sovereign Ledger").
3.  **Aesthetic Continuity:** Ensuring all live event artwork, skins, and UI updates adhere strictly to our **2.5D Pixar-Gothic** art direction style—combining blocky, tangible volumes with dark, atmospheric fantasy lighting.

3.  **Calendar System:** Schedule event timers using precise cron expressions to automatically run monthly crucible and world-boss campaigns, matching player time zones seamlessly.
