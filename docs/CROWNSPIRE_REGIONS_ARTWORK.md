## SECTION II: GENERAL LANDSCAPE PAINTING & GODOT LOADING ENGINE BLUEPRINT
Loading screens inside our web and Godot workspace bind to coordinate maps, triggering specific visual cards based on which region sector the player's capital resides:
1.  **Region Boundaries Checks:** The game engine tracks coordinates $(X, Y)$ during data loading sequences, querying corresponding assets `/assets/loading/<region_key>.png`.
2.  **Color Grade & LUT Alignment:** Every loading art follows strict Color Lookup Table (LUT) profiles to ensure visual unity across all devices (deep blacks, rich saturated accents, and low eye-strain values).
3.  **Visual Hierarchy Scaling:** Matte designs place large, dark silhouettes in the foreground ($20\%$ boundary) to create a sense of scale and depth, drawing the user's eyes toward the vibrant, glowing spires in the distance.
The final coordinate frameworks are synchronized with `/src/utils/cityLayout.ts` data properties. Let's execute compile and build checklists safely!
