## ART DIRECTION: THE "PIXAR-GOTHIC" BEAST DESIGN SCHEME
The monsters of **Crownspire** inhabit a dark, runic fantasy world, yet their visual design is constructed with the charming, high-readability rules of modern 3D feature films. By blending stylized "toy-like" proportions with ominous gothic details, we create a distinct, highly readable artistic trademark.

### Core Visual Directives
1.  **Exaggerated Volumetric Shapes:** Heads, claws, weapons, horns, and spines are modeled with extra fatness and simplified silhouettes. Avoid messy realistic hair or hyper-detailed fur shaders in favor of stylized, blocky hair clumps, rounded spikes, and hand-painted planes.
2.  **Luminescent Core Focus (Weakpoints):** Every creature features a distinct magical focal point (glowing crystal chest-cores, flaming crown-horns, neon-tinted eyes, or runic markings) to serve as a clear gameplay target.
3.  **Kinetic Color Theory:** 
    *   **Frost-types:** Translucent ice blues, cyan mists, and white snow drift shaders.
    *   **Fire/Cinder-types:** Dark charcoal, obsidian, bright magma orange, and volcano reds.
    *   **Swamp/Mire-types:** Murky olive greens, acidic lime glows, and muddy browns.
    *   **Shadow/Void-types:** Dark amethyst purples, deep blacks, and magenta energy arcs.
4.  **Isometric Battle Card Scaling:** Assets should be positioned correctly on an isometric map layer with a soft drop-shadow at their feet, and their bounds must accommodate action animations without cropping.

```

   lvl 1-10 (Basic/Feral)        lvl 21-30 (Frenzied/Wild)       lvl 41-50 (Corrupted/Dreaded)
```

## SECTION IV: TECHNICAL EXPORT & SPRITE PACKAGING RULES
To support the Godot tilemap rendering pipelines and responsive React web layout:
1.  **Isometric Anchor Point:** Align the pivot point of all creature models to the exact bottom-center (at the base tile level), ensuring proper depth sorting with adjacent trees, player units, and stronghold walls on the map canvas.
2.  **Sprite Canvas Sizing:** Single common monsters use standard $128 \times 128\text{px}$ sprite sheets. Elites use $256 \times 256\text{px}$ bounds, and World Bosses occupy a dedicated $512 \times 512\text{px}$ quadrant field.
3.  **UI Portrait Framing:** Each monster requires a square $1:1$ icon cropped close to its head, highlighting its unique eye and crown glow colors to maximize gameplay clarity in campaign hunt tabs.
