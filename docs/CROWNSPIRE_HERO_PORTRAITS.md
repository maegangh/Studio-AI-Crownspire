## ART DIRECTION OVERVIEW: THE "PIXAR-GOTHIC" STYLE
This document defines the visual guidelines and artistic prompts to render the complete hero roster of **Crownspire** in a stylized, high-fidelity **2.5D Pixar/DreamWorks style**. The goal is to balance the dark gothic tone of the Crownspire universe (slate greys, coppers, and twilight magic) with the warm, expressive character designs and exaggerated, charming proportions characteristic of modern animated films.

### Core Artistic Directives
1.  **Exaggerated Expressiveness:** Focus on large, emotionally communicative eyes, strong gestural silhouettes, and soft, clean facial planes that read beautifully on mobile displays.
2.  **Tactile Textures:** Highly detailed and contrasting materials. Fabric, metal, and fur should have tangible textures: brushed copper, scratched slate armor, soft wool linings, and glowing celestial star-threads.
3.  **Dynamic Runic Lighting:** A primary rim light of magical energy (usually cold cyan, warm copper, or deep lavender) casting soft, painterly ambient occlusion shadows to pop the character from the dark backgrounds.
