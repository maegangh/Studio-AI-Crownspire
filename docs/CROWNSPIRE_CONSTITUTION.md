# THE CROWNSPIRE CONSTITUTION
## Version 1.0.0 (Permanent Sovereign Charter)
### Authored by: Creative Directorate, Crownspire Studios

---

## FOREWORD: THE INVIOLABLE BOUNDARY

The **Crownspire Design Bible** defines *how* the systems of Aethelgard are constructed, how numbers scale, and how models are rendered. 

The **Crownspire Constitution**, however, is our defensive wall. It defines what Crownspire **will NEVER become**. 

Live-service products are vulnerable to slow, systematic degradation. Under the pressure of short-term monetization peaks, shifting team rosters, market trends, and creative fatigue, applications often sacrifice their unique identities to become generic, trend-chasing commodities. This Constitution exists to prevent that decay. 

Every developer, artist, designer, writer, and artificial assistant working on Crownspire must treat this document as an inviolable contract. If a proposed feature, asset, character, or line of code violates a rule in this Constitution, it is **vetoed immediately**, without exception and regardless of potential short-term financial gains.

We prioritize permanent consistency over fleeting trends. We preserve the crown.

## CHAPTER 1: CORE IDENTITY

### RULE 1.1: SOVEREIGNTY, NOT VAGABONDING (A FOCUS ON REIGN AND DUTY)
*   **The Rule:** Crownspire is a game about rulers, keepers, and the defense of sacred territory. The player is a **Sovereign** bound by duty to their realm, not a wandering sellsword, a rogue survivor, or a lawless pioneer. Every system, mechanic, and narrative beat must reinforce the immense weight of the crown, territorial order, and majestic leadership.
*   **Why It Exists:** To separate Crownspire from standard survival or low-stakes fantasy games. Players must feel a deep sense of permanent responsibility and institutional power, which drives long-term strategic alliance building and territory defense.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   The player upgrading their Sovereign Keep to protect citizens from Void Rifts.
    *   Story arcs where characters must resolve regional trade disputes and seal covenants.
    *   Alliance-wide construction of territorial Outposts to claim resource nodes under a unified border.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Adding survival mechanics like hunger, thirst, or building temporary primitive wood shelters.
    *   Allowing the player to discard their castle to become a "roving bandit" with no permanent city anchor.
    *   Narratives focusing on "ragtag groups of rebels" fighting for survival with no institutional or sovereign goals.

### RULE 1.2: MAJESTIC GRAVITY, NOT PLAYFUL WHIMSY
*   **The Rule:** The tone of the Crownspire universe is defined by **Majestic Gravity**—a proud, serious, and mythic atmosphere. It must never devolve into lighthearted whimsy, slapstick humor, fourth-wall breaks, or cartoonish parody. The stakes are geological; the aesthetic is legendary.
*   **Why It Exists:** High-saturation, cartoonish humor dilutes the narrative threat of the corrupting Void and diminishes the value of the players' achievements. A majestic tone elevates the premium feel of the product, attracting dedicated strategy players.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Grizzled veterans discussing troop deployment with measured, archaic vocabulary.
    *   Visual representation of the Void as an oppressive, crystalline, silent corruption.
    *   Musical scores utilizing heavy brass, pipe organs, and choral chants to emphasize weight.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Adding cute, talking-animal sidekicks who crack modern jokes during tense battles.
    *   Introducing "silly" emotes, dance animations (e.g., dabbing), or comical weapon skins (e.g., foam hammers).
    *   Using puns, internet memes, or pop-culture quips in quest dialogue.

## CHAPTER 2: ART DIRECTION

### RULE 2.1: HAND-PAINTED PHYSICAL PBR, NOT FLAT PROCEDURALISM OR HIGH-POLY REALISM
*   **The Rule:** The art style of Crownspire is strictly **High-Fidelity Stylized 3D Fantasy**. Surfaces must blend hand-painted artistic textures with physical, realistic material reactions (roughness, metallicity, normal maps). We will never use flat, unshaded procedural textures, nor will we chase hyper-photorealistic, high-polygon scans that look clinical and aging.
*   **Why It Exists:** Hand-painted PBR creates a unique, touchable, and timeless "toy-box" aesthetic that is highly readable on mobile screens and does not age rapidly like photorealism.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Stone walls featuring visible, hand-painted moss, cracked grout, and wind-blown dust reflecting light with subtle normal maps.
    *   Gold armor trims with hand-painted bevel highlights combined with physical, brushed metallic specular reflections.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Using raw, photorealistic Megascans textures that clash with stylized hero models.
    *   Applying flat, single-color flat-shaded textures with zero material data, resulting in a cheap, "plastic-toy" appearance.
    *   Procedurally tiling textures without custom, hand-painted ambient occlusion bakes in the cavities.

### RULE 2.2: THE ROYAL 60-30-10 PALETTE INTEGRITY, Not NEON/RAINBOW CLUTTER
*   **The Rule:** All scenes, interfaces, and characters must adhere strictly to the **60-30-10 Color Ratio**:
    *   **60% Dominant:** Slate, Charcoal, and Alabaster (Geological and structural grounding).
    *   **30% Secondary:** Burnished Copper, Gold, and Royal Purple (Sovereign prestige).
    *   **10% Accent:** Luminescent Crystal Glow, Teal, or Amber (High-magic energy).
    We will never allow visual designs that disintegrate into a chaotic rainbow of high-saturation neon colors.
*   **Why It Exists:** Color discipline is the fastest way to convey a premium, high-contrast, professional brand identity. Restricting the palette preserves readability and ensures that rare glowing accents pop dramatically.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   A hero wearing deep charcoal slate armor (60%), trimmed in antiqued copper with purple velvet lining (30%), wielding a scepter with a pulsing cyan crystal core (10%).
    *   A city block dominated by white alabaster and dark slate roofs, connected by copper runic conductors feeding a levitating purple anchor.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   A character wearing neon green armor, bright red capes, and yellow boots simultaneously.
    *   A spell effect that emits rainbow-colored, high-saturation sparkles across the entire screen.
    *   A building featuring bright blue, pink, and orange painted walls with no dominant anchoring slate or stone.

## CHAPTER 3: HERO DESIGN

### RULE 3.1: ANCHORS OF STATEHOOD, NOT EXPENDABLE MERCENARIES OR MEME CHARACTERS
*   **The Rule:** Every Hero must be an **Anchor of Sovereignty**—a designated leader representing an established faction, a royal house, or an ancient covenant. We will never introduce nameless mercenaries, generic soldiers, joke characters, or self-insert crossovers that do not carry narrative and strategic weight.
*   **Why It Exists:** To maintain the player's respect for the hero roster. If heroes are disposable or comical, players lose emotional attachment and strategic incentive to invest in their progression.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Sovereign Maegan Pringle, carrying the weight of the First Unification and leading the Aethelgard Hegemony.
    *   Dominic, a grizzled general who plunged his ironbrand into lava veins to protect his legions.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   "Drunk Blacksmith Bob," a comical hero whose active skill is throwing beer mugs at enemies.
    *   Collaborations featuring modern, non-fantasy characters (e.g., adding a sci-fi cyber-soldier as a playable hero).
    *   "Generic Spearman Unit #4" being elevated to hero status with zero personal lore or unique visual anchor.

### RULE 3.2: HEROIC PROPORTIONS AND SILHOUETTE RECOGNITION, NOT UNIFORM GEOMETRY
*   **The Rule:** Heroes must be designed with heroic, semi-stylized proportions (7.5 to 8 heads tall), with hands, feet, and key weapons scaled up by approximately $15\%$ to ensure readability. Furthermore, every hero's untextured black silhouette **must** be instantly recognizable from their signature weapon or asymmetrical design.
*   **Why It Exists:** On mobile screens, uniform, realistically proportioned characters blend together, destroying combat readability and visual charm. Asymmetry and weapon emphasis prevent the roster from feeling like generic "swords-and-shields" clones.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Dominic’s massive, asymmetrical dragon-scale pauldron on his left shoulder, contrasting with a sleek, fabric-wrapped right arm.
    *   Lorelai's silhouette being defined by her large, floating, rotating runic compass disk.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Designing all knights with identical, symmetrical steel plate armor, differing only by their shield emblems.
    *   Using realistic, standard human body ratios that make weapons look like thin, unreadable lines at $50\%$ zoom.
    *   Failing a silhouette test where a sword-wielding hero cannot be distinguished from three other sword-wielding heroes when blacked out.

## CHAPTER 4: CROWNMARKS

### RULE 4.1: RELICS OF ANCIENT TREATIES, NOT STAT-STICK COMMODITIES
*   **The Rule:** A Crownmark is a holy, historical relic of Aethelgard. It is not "generic loot." It must have an anti-generic name, a detailed historical origin entry in the Citadel Codex Museum, and a physical design incorporating weathered slate, copper conduits, and a pulsing crystalline core.
*   **Why It Exists:** If gear feels generic (e.g., "Sword +1"), it loses all aspirational value. Treating gear as historical relics turns progression into an engaging collection journey, boosting player retention.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   *Maegan's Founders' Scepter:* A Weapon slot Crownmark featuring floating amethyst shards bound by copper conductors, forged to seal the first peace.
    *   *Lumi's Frostbloom Pendant:* An Accessory slot Crownmark containing raw, frozen ice-crystals from the Frostbound peaks.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   "Iron Sword" or "Magic Ring" with standard, generic item descriptions.
    *   An accessory that looks like a modern wristwatch or a sci-fi laser pointer.
    *   Implementing a gear system where players farm hundreds of identical-looking rings with minor stat differences, cluttering inventory.

### RULE 4.2: STRICT SIGNATURE RESONANCE, NOT GENERIC SWAP-AND-PLAY GEAR
*   **The Rule:** Every Crownmark has a designated **Signature Hero**. When equipped by this hero, the item achieves **Resonance**, unlocking its passive skills and visual aura shaders. If equipped on a non-signature hero, a magical dampening seal is applied: the item suffers a flat **-40% penalty** to all base stats, and all levels of its passive skill nodes are **completely deactivated**.
*   **Why It Exists:** To prevent the "one-set-dominates-all" meta. If players can easily swap a single high-tier gear set across their entire roster, they have zero incentive to collect and upgrade other Crownmarks. This rule enforces roster diversification and long-term progression depth.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Equipping the *Crimson Ironbrand* on Dominic grants $100\%$ stats, ignites a flowing red magma shader on his model, and unlocks the "Thermal Magma Rupture" passive.
    *   Equipping the *Crimson Ironbrand* on Maegan triggers a red warning: "-40% Attunement Penalty applied. Passives locked."
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Allowing players to buy a "Universal Resonance Key" that bypasses the non-signature penalty.
    *   Designing Crownmarks that have "generic" signature statuses (e.g., "Signature to any Marksman class"), bypassing character-specific bonds.
    *   Removing the stat penalty to simplify the gear screen for casual players.

## CHAPTER 5: BUILDINGS & CITIES

### RULE 5.1: HIGH-SOARING ORDER AND ENERGY INTEGRATION, NOT CHAOTIC SPRAWL OR CYBERPUNK
*   **The Rule:** Crownspire’s cities represent the ultimate triumph of order. Architecture must be defined by soaring white alabaster plaster, weathered white-marble tiles, burnished copper frames, gothic geometry (pointed arches, buttresses), and visible integration with crystal power grids (copper conductors and runic bands leading to levitating purple crystal anchors). We will never permit chaotic, messy medieval mud slums or high-tech, neon-lit cyberpunk structures.
*   **Why It Exists:** Visual consistency is critical for the sovereign fantasy theme. If players can build chaotic, ungrounded slums or high-tech factories, the world’s artistic cohesion collapses, destroying the feeling of order.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   The Barracks featuring towering white-marble arches, reinforced with copper conductors that glow purple to train gargoyles.
    *   A Keep featuring soaring spires with levitating crystal crowns humming with celestial power.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Adding modular building skins that turn the city into a futuristic cyber-base with laser defense grids.
    *   Allowing players to build wooden, muddy, unstructured shacks with zero alabaster or copper framing.
    *   Introducing steam-powered, smoking coal factories that clash with the clean, runic crystal-conduction fantasy.

### RULE 5.2: EXAGGERATED SILHOUETTE AND HEAVY FOUNDATION GROUNDING, NOT FLAT CUBES
*   **The Rule:** Every building must look massive and physically heavy. Major structures (Keep, Forge, Spire) must feature exaggerated, distinctive roof silhouettes to preserve readability on the world map. Furthermore, the base of every wall must feature dark, weathered stone masonry that bleeds organically into the terrain with hand-painted dirt and grass tufts. We will never use flat, uniform cube buildings that float above the terrain.
*   **Why It Exists:** On a crowded world map, buildings must be instantly recognizable from their shape alone. Proper grounding prevents buildings from looking like cheap, floating assets dropped onto a flat canvas.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   The Forge featuring a massive, asymmetrical chimney framed by thick copper rivets, anchored by a wide base of dark, weathered slate that merges seamlessly with the surrounding grass.
    *   The Spire having a sharply pointed, vertical silhouette that dominates adjacent resource fields.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Designing buildings as uniform rectangular blocks with slightly different roof colors.
    *   Assets having perfectly clean, sharp bottom edges that sit flatly on the terrain, creating a visible "seam" that looks ungrounded.
    *   Buildings with thin, fragile supports that look physically incapable of holding up heavy alabaster towers.

## CHAPTER 6: WORLD & LORE

### RULE 6.1: RIGID CLASSICAL DIGNITY, NOT MODERN POP-CULTURE QUIPS OR SLANG
*   **The Rule:** All dialogue, lore codices, and interface text must use a measured, archaic, and dignified vocabulary. We will never allow modern casual contractions, contemporary slang, or references to real-world politics, trends, or internet memes.
*   **Why It Exists:** Language is the silent foundation of world-building. Modern slang instantly shatters the immersion of a proud, mythical fantasy, making the world feel cheap and artificial.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   *"By the copper seals of Aethelgard, we stand."*
    *   *"The heavy slate of our walls shall outlast the hollow void."*
    *   *"Speak no more of retreat, Commander; our duty is carved into the very stone of the spire."*
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   *"Let's crush these Void monsters, guys!"*
    *   *"Dominic is literally the GOAT, no cap."*
    *   *"This battle is going to be epic! Check out my new gear."*

### RULE 6.2: THE CENTER-BOUND THREAT (GEOLOGICAL SCALE), NOT INFINITE UNIFORM GRIDS
*   **The Rule:** The world map is not an infinite, uniform playground. It is a strictly structured coordinate grid centered on the **Throne of Aethelgard $(600, 600)$**. As players travel from the outer rings (radius > 450) to the core (radius < 200), the world undergoes geological transitions: resources shift from basic Wheat/Wood to elite Runic Iron, and PvE threats escalate from simple Wildlings to colossal Void Rifts and Celestial World Bosses.
*   **Why It Exists:** To create a natural, geographical endgame. If the world is uniform, players have no incentive to coordinate with alliances, push territorial lines inward, or engage in high-stakes capital wars.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Alliances establishing territorial fort networks to secure elite Runic Iron deposits near the middle ring.
    *   World Bosses appearing exclusively in the high-gravity core zones surrounding the capital.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Allowing players to spawn directly next to the central capital at Keep Level 1.
    *   Placing high-tier Runic Iron veins randomly in the starting outer ring, removing the geographical progression.
    *   Generating uniform "sandbox biomes" that repeat endlessly with no central geographic focal point.

## CHAPTER 7: GAMEPLAY PHILOSOPHY

### RULE 7.1: REAL-TIME TACTICAL MOBILITY, NOT STATIC AUTO-PATHS OR TELEPORTING MARCHES
*   **The Rule:** Combat and movement on Aethelgard's map must use **Real-Time Marching**. Players can manually redirect, pincer, or retreat their marching armies on the coordinate grid at any time. We will never use static, linear auto-paths, turn-based map grids, or instant-teleportation combat.
*   **Why It Exists:** Strategy games live or die by tactical agency. Real-time manual marching enables high-skill plays, surprising ambushes, and cooperative alliance maneuvers, making the world feel alive and highly competitive.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   A player manually ordering a marching Cavalry legion to flank a squishy Marksmen legion from behind.
    *   Using speed-up tokens to accelerate a march line to reinforce an ally’s keep before an enemy siege lands.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Clicking an enemy and watching the army instantly teleport to the coordinate to display a text combat report.
    *   Armies locked to linear "highways" between nodes, unable to deviate or retreat mid-march.
    *   Replacing manual real-time grid movement with a flat, turn-based campaign board.

### RULE 7.2: STRATEGY-GATED PvE & LOSS-MITIGATED PvP, NOT LINEAR STAT-CHECKING OR TOTAL ROSTER WIPES
*   **The Rule:** 
    *   **PvE:** High-tier monsters and Void Rifts must introduce complex behaviors (such as shields countered only by specific troop classes or bosses charging ranged lines) rather than simple inflated health bars.
    *   **PvP:** To preserve the player base, $80\%$ of casualties from PvP clashes are sent to the Keep's **Healer's Sanctuary** to recover using Wheat, rather than dying permanently. Troops only die permanently if the sanctuary's capacity is exceeded. We will **never** allow players to suffer total roster wipes that erase months of progress in a single offline raid.
*   **Why It Exists:** Stat-gated PvE is boring and encourages mindless spending. Total roster wipes in PvP destroy player retention, turning the game into a hostile environment where only the highest spenders can survive. Loss mitigation encourages active, fearless PvP participation.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   A World Boss that deflects all magic projectiles unless staggered by heavy Gargoyle ground-pounds.
    *   A player returning after an alliance siege to find their barracks empty, but their Healer's Sanctuary full of troops ready to be recovered.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   A boss that does nothing but deal $500\%$ more damage at level 10 than level 9, requiring players to buy gear to pass.
    *   Allowing an enemy player to completely delete a player's army permanently while they are offline, forcing them to start recruitment from Keep Level 1.
    *   Removing the Healer's Sanctuary to encourage "hardcore survival" mechanics.

## CHAPTER 8: ECONOMY & MONETIZATION

### RULE 8.1: ETHICAL PROGRESSION ACCELERATION, NOT PAYWALLED EXCLUSIVES OR PREDATORY GAMBLE TRAPS
*   **The Rule:** Monetization must focus exclusively on **Progression Acceleration, Premium QoL Convenience, and High-Status Cosmetics**. We will **never** lock exclusive gameplay content, core heroes, high-tier Crownmarks, or vital stat upgrades behind paywalls. Furthermore, failing upgrades or breakthroughs will **never** destroy the items or degrade their stats.
*   **Why It Exists:** Paywalled power destroys competitive integrity and alienates free-to-play players, who are the essential "content" for active servers. Upgrade destruction is a predatory mechanic that causes severe player frustration and churn.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Selling Speed-Up tokens that compress the time needed to upgrade the Keep from 3 days to instant.
    *   A Star Breakthrough that refunds 100% of its rare fragments and applies a permanent $+15\%$ success pity modifier on failure.
    *   Providing a 3:1 Omni-Shard exchange in the forge so players can grind for specific legendary Crownmark fragments.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Selling an "S-Tier Legendary Hero" that can *only* be acquired via real-money purchases and cannot be summoned using in-game keys.
    *   A weapon upgrade system where a failed attempt has a $5\%$ chance to permanently shatter the Crownmark, deleting it from the player's inventory.
    *   Locking advanced military research trees behind a premium purchase gate.

### RULE 8.2: VIP ACCRUAL THROUGH STRATEGIC ACTION, NOT SOLELY CASH-GATED PRIVILEGE
*   **The Rule:** The VIP Sovereign system must provide a visible, active path for Free-to-Play players to accrue experience points (consecutive daily logins, completing global alliance events, and clearing server milestones). While spenders can purchase VIP points directly, VIP levels must never grant exclusive, raw combat stat multipliers that cannot be matched by strategic army composition or high-tier Crownmark resonance.
*   **Why It Exists:** A completely cash-gated VIP system creates a class divide that kills server communities. Giving F2P players a path to earn VIP benefits rewards loyalty and maintains server balance.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   A player receiving 100 VIP Experience points daily for maintaining a 30-day login streak.
    *   VIP Tier 10 granting $+2$ concurrent building queues and a daily free Gacha key, but zero raw "+% Army Damage" buffs.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   VIP points only obtainable via direct credit card transactions.
    *   VIP Level 15 granting a permanent "+30% Damage" multiplier to all units, making spenders mathematically invincible against F2P tactics.
    *   Allowing players to buy VIP ranks that bypass the maximum Keep level limitations.

## CHAPTER 9: UI & UX

### RULE 9.1: THE SOVEREIGN OBSIDIAN CANVAS (DEEP MATTE DARK UI), NOT HIGH-SATURATION CARTESIAN BUTTONS
*   **The Rule:** The user interface is a strictly designed **Sovereign Obsidian Canvas**. Panels must use deep, matte dark-slate and weathered charcoal backings ($85\%$ opacity), framed by $1\text{px}$ gold or copper trim lines, and subtle $6^{\circ}$ crystalline corner facets. We will **never** use high-saturation cartesian buttons, flat white panels, or neon-blue/neon-yellow modern sci-fi framing.
*   **Why It Exists:** The dark-slate and copper framing reinforces the physical, ancient, and majestic tone of Crownspire. Flat white or neon-blue sci-fi elements destroy the fantasy immersion, looking cheap and out of place.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   The Hero Sanctuary Panel showing a dark-slate backing, with copper borders, golden action buttons, and generous negative space to focus on the 3D model.
    *   Inventory slots framed in deep basalt with active purple runes for Epic items, and burnished copper filigree for Legendary items.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   A modern, flat white settings menu with solid black Helvetica text and bright blue flat buttons.
    *   Using high-saturation neon yellow for normal buttons and bright cyan for progress bars, turning the screen into a chaotic HUD.
    *   Adding futuristic digital sound effects (e.g., electronic beeps and sci-fi lasers) to interface clicks.

### RULE 9.2: TYPE HIERARCHY AND DIVISIBLE GRIDS, NOT FONT SOUP OR DENSE TEXT BLOCKS
*   **The Rule:** Interface typography is restricted to three families: **Space Grotesk** for heavy display headers, **JetBrains Mono** for data and quantities, and **Inter** for body text. Spacing must strictly follow the **8dp Grid Standard** (all paddings and offsets divisible by 8). Modals must preserve at least **$24\%$ negative space** to ensure premium breathing room.
*   **Why It Exists:** To prevent "font soup" and visual clutter. Consistent typography and clean grid alignments are the markers of high-end, professional UX design, enhancing readability and minimizing cognitive fatigue.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   A Level-Up Modal centering the hero's name in Space Grotesk, displaying stat changes in aligned columns of JetBrains Mono, and using an exact $16\text{dp}$ padding around the panels.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Mixing Arial, Times New Roman, and Comic Sans on the same character sheet.
    *   Packing 40 lines of tiny, unaligned description text into a single panel with zero negative space or margins.
    *   Using arbitrary padding values (e.g., $11\text{px}$, $23\text{px}$) that break grid alignment on different mobile screens.

## CHAPTER 10: TECHNICAL PHILOSOPHY

### RULE 10.1: LOGIC-VIEW DECOUPLING AND DATA-DRIVEN JSON CONFIGURATIONS, NOT MONOLITHIC HARDCODING
*   **The Rule:** 
    *   **Architecture:** All models, calculations, and data states must be completely decoupled from visual representation. Game logic must never directly reference visual nodes, camera arrays, or UI elements. Communication flows upwards via Godot’s **Signal-Observer Pattern** and downwards via properties.
    *   **Data:** No game statistics, level multipliers, construction timers, or gacha weights may be hardcoded into script files. Everything must be loaded at runtime from structured **JSON configurations**.
*   **Why It Exists:** Monolithic hardcoding makes live-service updates extremely fragile, leading to regression bugs. Decoupled architecture and data-driven configs allow designers to adjust stats and push updates without touching the core game engine.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   A `DataManager` script that reads `res://data/crownmarks.json` at boot and caches the values in a dictionary.
    *   A `SovereignKeep` data model that calculates construction speeds and emits a `keep_upgrade_completed` signal, which the visual keep component listens to.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Writing `if current_level == 12: max_hp = 1450` directly inside a Hero GDScript.
    *   A UI button script that directly references and modifies an army's coordinate variable via `get_node("/root/WorldMap/Army").position += Vector2(10, 10)`.
    *   Hardcoding gacha drop chances inside the server's transaction script.

### RULE 10.2: THREADED INGESTION & OBJECT POOLING, NOT GC-SPIKING DYNAMIC INSTANTIATION
*   **The Rule:** The client must maintain a consistent 60 FPS on mid-tier mobile hardware. We will **never** instantiate and free node hierarchies (like projectiles, damage numbers, and particle emitters) dynamically during active gameplay. All temporary visual effects must be managed by pre-allocated **Object Pools**. Furthermore, viewport loading must occur on dedicated worker threads to prevent UI stuttering.
*   **Why It Exists:** Dynamic instantiation triggers heavy garbage collection spikes, causing noticeable framerate drops ("stuttering") on mobile devices during intense combat, destroying the premium experience.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   A `ProjectileManager` that instantiates 100 arrow nodes on boot, hiding them, and recycling them during battles.
    *   Loading the massive world map coordinates in the background while displaying an animated cinematic transition.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Calling `instance()` and `queue_free()` on 50 projectile nodes every second during a siege.
    *   Freezing the entire main thread for 3 seconds while loading a character's inventory panel.
    *   Running heavy database queries inside the `_process(delta)` frame loop.

## CHAPTER 11: LIVE SERVICE PHILOSOPHY

### RULE 11.1: HORIZONTAL META EXPANSION, NOT VERTICAL POWER-CREEP ERASURE
*   **The Rule:** Live-ops updates and new season releases must focus on **Horizontal Variety** (introducing new combat synergies, regional mechanics, unique status interactions, and alternative team builds) rather than simple vertical power creep (releasing a new tier of gear that renders all previous progression useless).
*   **Why It Exists:** Rapid vertical power creep devalues player effort and investment, destroying player trust and causing massive churn among veteran players. Horizontal expansion maintains a healthy, diverse meta where older assets remain viable.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   A new season introducing a "Gloomveil Decay" mechanic, making shield-reversing Crownmarks highly strategic without increasing raw attack stats.
    *   Releasing a new hero who specializes in counter-attacking high-speed Drakeriders, altering the active PvP meta.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Releasing "Tier 6 Troops" that cost the same as Tier 5 but have $+50\%$ flat stats, rendering Tier 5 obsolete.
    *   Introducing a new Crownmark set that has identical stats to old sets but includes a flat "+30% Attack" passive.
    *   Increasing maximum level caps without adding new mechanics or horizontal skill choices.

### RULE 11.2: SEASONAL ROTATION AND MAP SHIFTING, NOT STALE SERVER STAGNATION
*   **The Rule:** To maintain long-term retention, servers must operate on structured seasons. When a new season begins, the central Spires are "Re-Sealed," temporarily locking maximum caps, and new localized Void Rift zones are injected across coordinates, forcing alliances to relocate their territories. We will **never** allow a server to sit in permanent stagnation where one dominant alliance controls the capital indefinitely with no seasonal challenges.
*   **Why It Exists:** Strategy games become stale once a single alliance wins the capital and secures server dominance. Seasonal resets and map shifts shake up power dynamics, giving losing alliances a fresh start and providing winners with new challenges.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Season 2 launching, re-sealing the Throne of Aethelgard and introducing a frozen northern frontier that alliances must race to claim.
    *   Injecting shifting Void Rifts that require alliances to coordinate new border fort lines.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Leaving a server running for 12 months with no resets, updates, or geographical changes, allowing a single guild to farm resources unchallenged.
    *   Refusing to reset territorial controls because players "worked hard to build them," resulting in a frozen map.

## CHAPTER 12: FUTURE EXPANSION RULES

### RULE 12.1: ADAPTABLE MODULE INTEGRITY, NOT CORE-BREAKING SYSTEM HACKS
*   **The Rule:** All future system expansions (such as introducing a 6th "Signet Ring" slot, adding companion pets, or introducing dragon breeding) must be registered dynamically via dedicated adapters (e.g., `CrownmarkExpansionAdapter`). Developers are strictly forbidden from rewriting the core `crownmarks.json` database structure or hacking the base `SaveManager` schemas to accommodate seasonal features.
*   **Why It Exists:** Modifying core, stable database schemas for temporary or seasonal expansions introduces massive risks of profile corruption and regression bugs. Adapter patterns keep the core engine clean and maintainable.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Registering a new "Companion" system using an independent `CompanionData` module that hooks into the `EventChannel` without touching the base `Hero` class.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Directly editing the core `Hero` schema inside `DataManager` to add 15 new variables for a temporary winter event.
    *   Hacking the core battle resolver to hardcode a specific companion's damage formula instead of using the modular status effect register.

### RULE 12.2: THE CREATIVE DIRECTOR'S DOUBLE-GATE OF VETO, NOT TREND-CHASING FEATURE CREEP
*   **The Rule:** Any feature proposal that introduces modern trends (e.g., blockchain elements, virtual reality modes, casual match-3 minigames, unrelated viral trends) must pass a **Double-Gate of Veto**:
    1.  *Narrative Integrity:* Does this feature exist physically in the lore of Aethelgard and respect the Majestic Gravity of the world?
    2.  *Sovereign Vector:* Does this feature reinforce the player's role as a Sovereign defending their realm?
    If the answer to either gate is "No," the feature is **permanently rejected**.
*   **Why It Exists:** Feature creep is the silent killer of game design. Adding unrelated, trendy minigames or systems dilutes the core strategy gameplay, clutters the interface, and alienates our core player base.
*   **Examples of Compliance:**
    *   Rejecting a proposal to add an in-game "candy crush" minigame to speed up crop harvesting, replacing it instead with a strategic "Crop Irrigation" research node.
*   **Examples of Violation:**
    *   Integrating virtual-reality card battles that have no mechanical connection to the world map or troop marching systems.
    *   Adding an "idle pet runner" minigame where players control a running dog to collect coins, breaking the serious fantasy tone.

# THE 25 IMMUTABLE LAWS OF CROWNSPIRE

The twenty-five laws codified below represent the absolute boundaries of Crownspire’s design space. They are permanent, non-negotiable architectural walls. While game parameters, UI assets, and server coordinates may adapt to contemporary software paradigms, these laws are inviolable. They must be followed by every systems architect, database engineer, visual artist, and game writer under the authority of the Creative Directorate.

---

### LAW 1: THE MMO KINGDOM-BUILDING ANCHOR
Crownspire must always remain, at its core, a fantasy kingdom-building massively multiplayer online (MMO) strategy game. It shall never be mutated into an offline single-player RPG, a standalone match-3 puzzle game, a localized rogue-lite runner, or an isolated sandbox city builder. The game’s systems must permanently support and depend upon thousands of concurrent Sovereigns interacting, trading, and battling on a shared coordinate map.

### LAW 2: THE ROYAL CRYSTAL THEMATIC SOVEREIGNTY
The royal crystal fantasy aesthetic is our primary brand anchor and must never be abandoned or diluted. The visual harmony of white alabaster stone, weathered slate roofs, burnished copper circuitry, and pulsing amethyst/emerald power nodes is absolute. We will never introduce high-tech sci-fi cybernetics, contemporary realistic industrialism, cartoon steampunk gears, or high-saturation modern military aesthetics.

### LAW 3: HEROIC SINGULARITY AND SILHOUETTE IDENTITY
Every hero introduced to the roster must possess a completely unique visual silhouette, personality, and gameplay role. There shall be no uniform "pallet-swapped" clones or generic infantry units promoted to hero status. A player must be able to recognize any hero in under $100\text{ms}$ based solely on their asymmetrical outline and signature weapon, and each hero must offer a mechanical playstyle that cannot be perfectly duplicated by another character.

### LAW 4: THE INSEPARABLE RELIC BOND (CROWNMARK ATTUNEMENT)
Every Crownmark must be treated as a legendary, memorable historical relic linked directly to its designated Signature Hero. We will never permit generic "slot-filler" equipment or universal power-swapping sets. If a Crownmark is equipped by a non-signature hero, it must suffer a mandatory, flat $-40\%$ stat attenuation penalty, and its high-tier passive skills must be completely locked. Equipment is an extension of character lore, not a nameless stat-stick.

### LAW 5: MOBILE READABILITY OVER VISUAL COMPLEXITY
User interface design must always prioritize instantaneous readability under direct sunlight over unnecessary aesthetic complexity, excessive text blocks, or dense visual noise. Typography pairings are strictly gated (Space Grotesk for display, JetBrains Mono for data, Inter for body). Modals must preserve a minimum of $24\%$ negative space. A screen cluttered with small, hard-to-read numbers is a design failure.

### LAW 6: ORGANIC GAMEPLAY DEPTH BEFORE HORIZONTAL COMPLEXITY
New system features should deepen and enrich existing gameplay mechanics before introducing entirely new systems that require independent resource currencies. If an existing feature is stagnant, it must be refined, re-balanced, or integrated more tightly into the core loop. Adding secondary, unrequested mini-games or "systems-on-top-of-systems" to artificially drive daily metrics is strictly forbidden.

### LAW 7: PROGRESSION ACCELERATION, NOT INVESTED VALUE ERASURE
Monetization options must act purely as progression acceleration, convenience upgrades, or prestige cosmetics. They must never make a free-to-play or low-spending player's long-term time investment feel worthless. There shall be no premium-only stat boundaries or paywalls that block competitive mechanical viability. A player's deep strategic mastery and alliance coordination must always have the agency to overcome raw, uncoordinated financial spending.

### LAW 8: IDENTITY SOVEREIGNTY OVER TREND-CHASING COPYCATING
Every new feature, live-ops event, or social mechanic must reinforce Crownspire's unique identity as a majestic sovereign strategy game instead of mindlessly copying competitors. If a competitor introduces a popular feature (e.g., card-battlers or block-puzzles), we are forbidden from dropping it into Aethelgard as a quick cash-grab. We adapt strategic elements only if they can be executed through our majestic lens of territory defense and royal duty.

### LAW 9: THE GEOLOGICAL THREAT CURVE
The coordinate grid map must maintain a strict, geographic progression where threat levels and resource value scale exponentially as coordinates approach the central core $(600,600)$. The outer rings must remain a safe haven for beginners, while the inner rings must be high-conflict, resource-dense zones. We will never flatten this curve or randomize high-tier nodes to make the map uniformly accessible, as it destroys alliance-based territorial progression.

### LAW 10: ANTI-DESTRUCTION UPGRADE ASSURANCE
Upgrading, reinforcing, or breaking through Crownmarks, buildings, or heroes must never result in the physical destruction, level degradation, or regression of the asset. Failure during high-stakes Star Breakthroughs must apply a cumulative $+15\%$ success pity modifier and refund materials, rather than punishing the player with item deletion. Predatory gambling mechanics that wipe out weeks of player effort are permanently banned.

### LAW 11: MANUAL REAL-TIME TACTICAL MOBILITY
Army marches on the world map coordinate grid must use real-time pathing with absolute player control. Sovereigns must maintain the tactical freedom to redirect, intercept, pincer, or retreat their legions at any moment. We will never lock army movement to static linear paths, pre-determined auto-highways, or turn-based spatial grids. The map is a living, breathing chess board of active human decisions.

### LAW 12: THE HEALER'S SANCTUARY PvP PROTECTION SHIELD
To maintain player retention and encourage active map conflict, PvP combat casualties must be mitigated by the Keep’s Healer's Sanctuary. A minimum of $80\%$ of troops wounded during defensive sieges or resource skirmishes must be directed to the sanctuary to recover using soft currencies (Wheat and Gold), rather than dying permanently. Troops shall only experience permanent death if the sanctuary’s capacity is completely exceeded, preventing offline annihilation.

### LAW 13: THE SOVEREIGN OBSIDIAN CANVAS INTEGRITY
The user interface must strictly adhere to the "Sovereign Obsidian Canvas" visual theme—deep, matte dark-slate backings ($85\%$ opacity), copper and burnished gold thin borders, $6^{\circ}$ crystalline corner facets, and high-contrast silver-white text. We will never implement flat white light-mode themes, high-saturation cartoonish button cards, or futuristic neon-blue cyber grids.

### LAW 14: STRATEGY-GATED PvE DESIGN
PvE monsters, world bosses, and Void Rifts must be designed with complex tactical behaviors that challenge player intelligence and class synergy, rather than functioning as simple linear stat-checks. A higher-level boss must introduce active mechanics (e.g., class-based shields, positional charges, coordinate traps) that force alliance coordination and custom troop configurations, rather than just having inflated health bars.

### LAW 15: THE ROYAL 60-30-10 COLOR RATIO
Every single piece of concept art, 3D model, promotional banner, and interface viewport must strictly preserve the 60-30-10 color rule. Slate-grey, weathered basalt, and alabaster stone must form the 60% grounding foundation; copper, gold, and royal purple must form the 30% structural prestige; and luminescent crystal glow (amber, cyan, or amethyst) must be restricted to the 10% accent details. Rainbow-colored, high-saturation visual clutter is a vetoable offense.

### LAW 16: PHYSICAL MATERIAL PBR INTEGRITY
The 3D rendering pipeline must strictly blend hand-painted, stylized textures with physically accurate PBR material maps (roughness, metalness, and micro-normals). We will never accept flat, unshaded cartoon vectors, nor will we use raw, photorealistic, high-polygon scans. The game must maintain a tactile, touchable, "hand-carved toy-box" aesthetic that remains timeless.

### LAW 17: DECOUPLED LOGIC-VIEW ENGINEERING
The software architecture must maintain a strict, absolute separation of concerns between core game logic and visual viewport representations. Model scripts, database managers, and stat calculators must never directly reference spatial nodes, camera matrices, or UI nodes. Communication must flow strictly upwards via Godot's Signal-Observer pattern and downwards via state properties.

### LAW 18: DATA-DRIVEN CONFIGURATION DOMINANCE
Under no circumstances are gameplay numbers, level scales, construction speeds, gacha drop rates, or combat formulas to be hardcoded into engine scripts. All mechanical data must be read at runtime from structured JSON database files loaded by the central `DataManager`. This ensures that balance changes and live-ops content can be pushed safely without compiling core engine code.

### LAW 19: ACOUSTIC MATERIAL ANCHORING (HAPTIC AUDIO)
The soundscape of Crownspire must be anchored by high-quality, physical, instrumental acoustic recordings (anvil strikes, deep church bells, acoustic cellos, stone slides, and metallic clinks). We will never permit generic digital synthesizer sound effects, sci-fi computer beeps, or cartoonish comic sweeps in the sound design. Every audio cue must convey physical, geological weight.

### LAW 20: ACCESSIBILITY VISUAL SAFETY STANDARD
Crownspire must maintain global accessibility standards to ensure visual safety and inclusivity. All interface text must preserve a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against its backing panel. Visual settings must include high-contrast colorblind toggles that map rarity halos (Epic/Legendary) to distinct geometric runic glyphs, and high-frequency flickering animations must be strictly padded to prevent seizure triggers.

### LAW 21: NO ACTIVE GAMEPLAY THREAD FREEZING
The user experience must remain completely fluid, targeting a locked 60 FPS on mid-tier mobile hardware. Core gameplay loops are strictly forbidden from performing synchronous filesystem operations, heavy JSON parses, or un-pooled node instantiations. Projectiles, combat numbers, and particles must use pre-allocated object pools, and heavy viewport loads must occur on background worker threads.

### LAW 22: ADAPTABLE MODULAR INTEGRITY
All future game expansions must be integrated via dedicated adapters that hook into the central Event Channel without modifying stable core systems or core database schemas. We will never permit structural "hacks" or manual modifications to the base SaveManager or Hero data schemas for temporary seasonal events. The codebase must remain clean, modular, and maintainable.

### LAW 23: CONSECUTIVE LOG-IN VIP ACCRUAL
The VIP Sovereign system must remain accessible to all players. Active, free-to-play Sovereigns must be guaranteed a path to earn VIP experience points passively through consecutive daily logins, completing alliance activities, and achieving server milestones. The VIP system is a loyalty tracker, not an exclusive, paywalled spending tier.

### LAW 24: SEASONAL BOUNDARY RE-SEALING
To prevent permanent server stagnation and monopoly by a single dominant alliance, servers must operate on structured seasonal cycles. Every season must "Re-Seal" the central Spires, temporarily lock maximum progression caps, and inject randomized, shifting Void Rifts across map coordinates. This forces alliances to re-strategize, move borders, and allow rising guilds a fresh path to victory.

### LAW 25: THE CREATIVE DIRECTOR'S DOUBLE-GATE OF VETO
No feature, collaboration, system expansion, or design change shall be integrated into the product unless it passes the Double-Gate of Veto with absolute clarity:
1.  *The Narrative Gate:* Does this proposed asset or system exist physically in the lore of Aethelgard and fully respect the Majestic Gravity of our universe?
2.  *The Sovereign Gate:* Does this mechanic directly reinforce the player's core identity as a Sovereign ruling and defending their kingdom?
If the answer to either gate is "No," the proposal is **permanently vetoed**, regardless of immediate financial projection metrics.
